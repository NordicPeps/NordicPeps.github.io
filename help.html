Customer Support

Help Center

Need help with your order? Find answers to some of the most common questions below.

Can't see your order receipt?

If you have completed your order but can't find your receipt or confirmation email, please check your spam or junk folder. Sometimes automated emails can end up there.

Common Questions

I completed my order but didn't receive an email
First, check your spam or junk folder. If the email is still missing, make sure you entered the correct email address when placing your order. If you still need help, contact us using the email below.
Where can I find my order receipt?
Your order receipt is normally sent to the email address you provided during checkout. Please check your inbox as well as your spam or junk folder.
I entered the wrong email address
If you accidentally entered the wrong email address during checkout, contact our support team as soon as possible and include your order number so we can help you.
My payment went through but I have a problem with my order
Please contact us with your order number and explain the problem clearly. We'll look into the issue and help you as soon as possible.
How can I contact Nordic Peps?
For more complex questions or problems that aren't answered here, please email our support team at nordicpeptis@gmail.com.

Still need help?

If you have a more complex problem or your question isn't answered above, send us an email and we'll help you with your issue. NordicPeptis@gmail.com
